Travel to Victoria, BC



A flight to Victoria (YYJ) is the best option.

You will likely need a rental vehicle as its ~30min from the airport into Central Victoria and another ~30min to Hatley Castle from there.

There are plenty of ride-share options as well (uber, taxis).

Americans:
You do NOT need a visa to enter Canada.

There will be another ceremony in China!

This will be a mix of a traditional Chinese and Western wedding ceremony.
Primary language will be Mandarin.
Location: Zibo, Shandong province, China.
Date is pending but tentatively ~Chinese New Year 2028.

If you are interested, please let us know in the RSVP form.
For Canadians, a Visa is NOT required.
Approximate cost will vary (especially for Americans). Alberta will be ~150CAD.

Applications owned by Google and Meta are not functional.
This includes things like:
Facebook, Instagram, Whatsapp, Google Translate, Google Search, Google Maps, Gmail, etc.

For communication, I recommend WeChat. This is essentially Chinese Whatsapp.
For navigation, I recommend Apple Maps (for ios) and AMap (for android).
For translation, I recommend Papago or Baidu Translate. Many of the apps above have an English translation option already.
Most of these restrictions can be circumvented via a VPN.

VERY IMPORTANT! For payments please set up BOTH Alipay as well as WeChat Pay.
China does not do Credit/Debit card tap, so this will be your primary method of paying for goods & services.

Get an E-sim, would be best if it also comes with a Chinese phone number.
E-sims are a cost effective way to get roaming data. You can choose a roaming option from your normal cellular provider but theyre typically quite costly.

Travel to destination:
I strongly recommend you get a flight directly to Qingdao.
This normally requires you to have a layover (depending on where your origin country is) in South Korea, Japan, Vancouver
Other less convenient layovers could be in Beijing or Shanghai.
